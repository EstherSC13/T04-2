# Australian TV Energy Consumption & Market Analysis

## Data Story

### Target Audience
* **Who is the audience?**: Australian consumers looking to purchase a new television.
* **Characteristics**: They want affordable, energy-efficient, and appropriately sized TVs, and they prefer clear, visual insights over overly technical specifications.

### Key Questions & Findings

1. **What type of TV screen technologies are currently available in Australia and which are the most frequent?**
   * *Insight*: Most TVs in Australia are LCD, while LED and OLED options are less common.
2. **What screen sizes are available and which are most frequent?**
   * *Insight*: 65-inch TVs are the most common on the market, followed by mid-range sizes such as 55 to 85 inches.
3. **Which brands have the largest number of models?**
   * *Insight*: Samsung, LG, and Kogan have the largest number of models available on the Australian market.
4. **Which type of screen technology consumes the least amount of power?**
   * *Insight*: LCD TVs consume the least power, while OLEDs use slightly more but offer higher display performance.
5. **What is the relationship between screen size and power use?**
   * *Insight*: The larger the TV size, the higher the power usage. There is a clear positive correlation.
6. **What is the relationship between star rating and screen size?**
   * *Insight*: Star ratings are fairly distributed across all screen sizes; larger TVs can still be energy efficient if well designed.

### Conclusion
When choosing a TV, balancing size, brand, and efficiency is essential. A 65-inch LED TV represents a common and energy-smart option for most households.


## About the Data

* **Data Source**: Official Australian Government dataset containing energy consumption metrics, ratings, and specifications for television models sold in Australia.
* **Data Processing**: Data transformation and cleaning were conducted using KNIME Analytics Platform. Workflow steps included removing duplicates, handling missing attributes, converting screen dimensions from centimeters to inches, and aggregating metrics (e.g., median power and star ratings) for plotting.
* **Privacy**: The dataset exclusively contains publicly available product specifications and energy metrics; no private or personally identifiable information is included.
* **Accuracy and Limitations**: Missing or incomplete entries were removed to preserve data integrity. The dataset serves as a market snapshot, which may not capture newly released models or regional stock variations.
* **Ethics**: The dataset has been analyzed and presented objectively without manipulation, bias, or selective reporting favoring specific brands or technologies.
